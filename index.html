A minimal movie catalog

Imitation Game is a course project that models a small slice of a movie database: six related tables, static pages, and CSV-backed data. Browse movies and the people who act, direct, and write—once the data layer is connected, lists and detail views will populate the shells on this site.

Stack: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in the browser; relational design expressed as CSV files under assets/csv_files/.
